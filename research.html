Research Statement

Tendons are poorly vascularized tissues that connect muscle to bone, which limits their ability to regenerate after injury and leaves them to heal as scar tissue. Previous research has shown that mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) release anti-inflammatory and pro-regenerative factors that improve tendon healing in animal models. My future research aims to determine how treating injured tendons with MSC-derived factors affects collagen organization and scar formation during healing. These findings could support regenerative therapies that shorten recovery time and reduce reinjury for athletes and older adults.
